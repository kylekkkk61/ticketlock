TicketLock smart contract
{{icon:solidity:20:#111111}}{{icon:openzeppelin:20:#111111}}
Each ticket is an NFT (ERC-721). The contract is both the rule book and the record.
  • Events organiser and capacity
  • Owner of every ticket
  • Used or not and the time of entry
  • Counter that cancels old signatures
Rules it enforces
  • Only the platform can issue tickets
  • A ticket moves only with its owner's signature
  • Entry needs the owner and the organiser, once
Anyone can read all of it with a block explorer or our independent verifier, without asking TicketLock.
