TicketLock · customer journey

Buy, transfer, enter

What a customer does on their phone, and what the system does underneath. These are screens from the live app.
Buy
1
Pay at checkout
You: Sign in with an email code and press Simulate payment.
Underneath: The platform creates a new NFT ticket in your account and pays the network fee. It takes about two seconds.
Transfer
2
Send it to someone
You: Type their email, check the recipient, press Confirm transfer.
Underneath: Your confirmation is a digital signature. The contract moves the ticket only if that signature is valid.
Enter
3
Scan the door code
You: Press Enter event and point the camera at the organiser's code.
Underneath: The code only names the event. It holds no secret, so copying it gives nobody a ticket.
 
4
Confirm, show the code
You: Press Confirm entry and show the four characters to staff.
Underneath: You signed an entry permit that lasts two minutes. Nothing on the blockchain has changed yet.
 
5
Staff admit you
You: Staff match the code and press Admit. The ticket turns to Used.
Underneath: Both signatures reach the contract in one transaction. It marks the ticket used, for good.
What the customer never sees
A wallet app, a recovery phrase, a network fee or a blockchain address. The only extra step compared with an ordinary ticketing site is pressing confirm.
