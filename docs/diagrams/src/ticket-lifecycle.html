TicketLock · ticket lifecycle

A ticket has two states, and only moves forward

The contract records one fact about use: available or used. There is no way back.
Not issued
No ticket exists yet.
Capacity is fixed when the event is created.
issueTicket
the platform, after checkout
Available
Owned by one account.
Can be transferred or presented for entry.
Counter starts at 0.
transferWithPermit
the owner signs · new owner · counter +1
checkIn
owner and organiser both sign
Used
Entry time recorded by the network.
Stays with the holder as a record.
No transfer, no second entry, no reset.
Why a counter?
A signature approves one action at one counter value. When the ticket moves, the counter rises and every earlier signature is void, even if the ticket later returns to the same person.
Which happens first wins
If a transfer and an entry request are both waiting, the first one the network processes succeeds and the other is refused automatically.
Nothing is deleted
Used tickets are not destroyed. Each step stays on the public record with its time, its signers and its transaction.
