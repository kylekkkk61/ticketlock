TicketLock · smart contract

What the contract can do, and who may ask

One contract holds every event and ticket. Its functions fall into four groups by who is allowed to call them.
Platform only
Only TicketLock's issuer account.
createEvent
Registers an event with its organiser and capacity.
In the app: set up once per event, before sales.
issueTicket
Creates one ticket for a buyer. An order number works once, and issuing stops at capacity.
In the app: Simulate payment.
Needs signatures
Anyone may deliver it. The signatures decide.
transferWithPermit
Moves a ticket if its current owner signed this exact transfer to this exact recipient.
In the app: Confirm transfer.
checkIn
Marks a ticket used if its owner and the event's organiser both signed the same permit.
In the app: Confirm entry, then Admit.
Anyone can read
Free, no account needed.
getTicket · ticketsOf
A ticket's owner, event, used or not, and time of entry. All tickets of one account.
In the app: My tickets, the verifier.
getEvent
Organiser, capacity, tickets issued and checked in.
In the app: event page, organiser dashboard.
activityBlocks
Where on the chain a ticket's history is recorded.
In the app: On-chain proof.
Standard NFT functions
The ERC-721 standard every NFT follows.
ownerOf · balanceOf · tokenURI
Who owns a ticket, how many an account holds, and the ticket's name and image.
In the app: explorers and wallets can display our tickets.
transferFrom · approve
Ordinary NFT transfers by the owner. Our rules still apply to them.
In the app: not used; kept for compatibility.
Built into
every path
Every move cancels old signatures
Each ticket has a counter that rises on every transfer and at entry.
A used ticket is frozen
It cannot be transferred, admitted again or reset by anyone.
Signatures expire
Five minutes for a transfer, two minutes for entry.
